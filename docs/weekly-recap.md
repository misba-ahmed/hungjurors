# League HQ · Weekly Recap

`scripts/recap-live.js` replaces `hjRecapHTML` in `index.html` (swapped in by `scripts/prepare-site.mjs`, which also links `styles/weekly-recap.css`). The week selector, the `hjRecapStories` storylines, `hjRecapLoadExtra` (player pool, NFL scoreboard, transactions and the ESPN game summaries with play-by-play) and the live patching in `hjRefreshRecap` are the page's existing code and are unchanged.

The tab, top to bottom: a podium for the week's top three scores; the Weekly Awards grid, each with its real figure (biggest blowout and narrow victory by margin with both scores, over/underachiever as the signed gap to the starter projection, most/least efficient manager as a percentage with points left on the bench, highest points in a loss and lowest points in a win with the opponent's score and the margin); Slipped Away; The Final Blow; Team Performance bars (points scored against the best possible lineup, with result, opponent, all-play record and projection); Players of the Week and Benchwarmers of the Week as navy headshot cards (`hjRcPlayerCard`, photo inside the card) (top scorer per position, with the manager who started them, whose bench they sat on, or free agent, and the vs/@ opponent); False Starters (the five starters furthest under their projection, the first as a team-coloured feature card, each with its manager); Game Changer (the winning starter whose margin over projection flipped a result, with the matchup); then the storylines from `hjRecapStories` in plain white cards.

Slipped Away and The Final Blow come from a play-by-play score timeline (`hjRcSwings`). For each matchup, every starter's NFL game log is read; each play is matched to the player by ESPN's `F.Last` alias and scored (pass yards/TDs/INTs, receptions/yards/TDs, rushes, field goals by distance, extra points, two-point conversions), then each player's per-play increments are rescaled so they end exactly on the player's real fantasy total. D/ST units, kickers with no matched plays and unmatched players accrue evenly across their game. Plays carry ESPN's wallclock; plays without one borrow a time from their neighbours. Both sides' scores are then replayed in wall-clock order and a win chance is estimated at every play from locked points plus the remaining projection of each unfinished player (normal approximation, spread 0.6 × remaining projection). Slipped Away shows the losing side with the highest peak win chance, the lead at that moment, the opponent's players still to play and what they added from there, with a win-chance chart. The Final Blow is the point of no return: the last real fantasy play (a starter actually scoring, not a game-end or an evenly spread D/ST increment) after which the loser's win chance never got back above 5%, with the exact Central time, game, quarter/clock, play text, the win chance before and after, the score it created, who scored it and, when the loser's last game ended later, the time the matchup officially closed. Every matchup's point of no return is listed beneath it; Sunday-night and Monday-night ones are featured first. Both blocks wait until the week's game logs are loaded (the tab shows a loading note first), and a matchup is skipped if any scoring starter's game log is missing.
