# League HQ · Weekly Recap

`scripts/recap-live.js` replaces `hjRecapHTML` in `index.html` (swapped in by `scripts/prepare-site.mjs`, which also links `styles/weekly-recap.css`). The week selector, the `hjRecapStories` storylines, `hjRecapLoadExtra` (player pool, NFL scoreboard, transactions and the ESPN game summaries with play-by-play) and the live patching in `hjRefreshRecap` are the page's existing code and are unchanged.

The tab, top to bottom: a podium for the week's top three scores; the League Awards grid (biggest blowout, narrow victory, over/underachiever against starter projections, most/least efficient manager against the optimal lineup, highest points in a loss, lowest points in a win); Slipped Away; The Final Blow; Team Performance bars (points scored against the best possible lineup, with result, opponent, all-play record and projection); Players of the Week and Benchwarmers of the Week as the MVP-style headshot cards (top scorer per position, with the manager who started them, whose bench they sat on, or free agent, and the vs/@ opponent); False Starters (the three starters furthest under their projection, the first as a team-coloured feature card); Game Changer (the winning starter whose margin over projection flipped a result, with the matchup); then the storylines.

Slipped Away and The Final Blow come from a play-by-play score timeline (`hjRcSwings`). For each matchup, every starter's NFL game log is read; each play is matched to the player by ESPN's `F.Last` alias and scored (pass yards/TDs/INTs, receptions/yards/TDs, rushes, field goals by distance, extra points, two-point conversions), then each player's per-play increments are rescaled so they end exactly on the player's real fantasy total. D/ST units, kickers with no matched plays and unmatched players accrue evenly across their game. Plays carry ESPN's wallclock; plays without one borrow a time from their neighbours. Both sides' scores are then replayed in wall-clock order and a win chance is estimated at every play from locked points plus the remaining projection of each unfinished player (normal approximation, spread 0.6 × remaining projection). Slipped Away shows the losing side with the highest peak win chance, the lead at that moment, the opponent's players still to play and what they added from there, with a win-chance chart. The Final Blow finds the first play after which the loser had no players left and trailed: the exact Central time, game, quarter/clock and play text. Every matchup's elimination is listed beneath it; Sunday-night and Monday-night endings are featured first. Both blocks wait until the week's game logs are loaded (the tab shows a loading note first), and a matchup is skipped if any scoring starter's game log is missing.
