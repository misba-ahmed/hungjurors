# League HQ · Weekly Recap

`scripts/recap-live.js` replaces `hjRecapHTML` in `index.html` (swapped in by `scripts/prepare-site.mjs`, which also links `styles/weekly-recap.css`). The week selector, the `hjRecapStories` storylines, `hjRecapLoadExtra` (player pool, NFL scoreboard, transactions and the ESPN game summaries with play-by-play) and the live patching in `hjRefreshRecap` are the page's existing code and are unchanged.

The panel's generic 'Weekly Recap' head and week note are hidden by the stylesheet; the tab carries its own header and week selector. The tab, top to bottom: a podium for the week's top three scores; Primetime Sweat (for Sunday night and Monday night kickoff, each matchup's score and win chance at that moment, players still to play, and whether it was in doubt, safe or already final); the Weekly Awards grid (manager and figure on one line, each with its real figure (biggest blowout and narrow victory by margin with both scores, over/underachiever as the signed gap to the starter projection, most/least efficient manager as a percentage with points left on the bench, highest points in a loss and lowest points in a win with the opponent's score and the margin); Slipped Away; The Cellar (the three lowest scores sinking into a pit, with how far below the league average each finished); Movers (standings through the week with rank change since last week and W/L streaks); Team Performance bars (points scored against the best possible lineup, with result, opponent, all-play record and projection); Position Battles (per matchup, a six-cell strip showing who won QB, RB, WR, TE, K and D/ST and by how much); Players of the Week and Benchwarmers of the Week as navy headshot cards (`hjRcPlayerCard`, the photo rising out of the card top) (top scorer per position, with the manager who started them, whose bench they sat on, or free agent, and the vs/@ opponent); Waiver Wire (from the week's executed waiver and free-agent transactions: the added player who scored most, with who added him and whether he started, and the dropped player who scored most for someone else or unclaimed); False Starters (the five starters furthest under their projection, the first as a team-coloured feature card, each with its manager); Game Changer (the winning starter whose margin over projection flipped a result, with the matchup); Out of Character (the biggest rise and fall against a manager's own season average, with a season sparkline); then the storylines from `hjRecapStories` in plain white cards, each headed by the portraits of the managers it mentions, a kind-specific visual (`hjRcStoryVisual`: all-play faces, actual-vs-max bar, projection bars, share ring, streak dots, position strip, head-to-head, sparkline, game logos, half split or the player's headshot) and the evidence line split into a bulleted list.

Slipped Away comes from a play-by-play score timeline (`hjRcSwings`). For each matchup, every starter's NFL game log is read; each play is matched to the player by ESPN's `F.Last` alias and scored (pass yards/TDs/INTs, receptions/yards/TDs, rushes, field goals by distance, extra points, two-point conversions), then each player's per-play increments are rescaled so they end exactly on the player's real fantasy total. D/ST units, kickers with no matched plays and unmatched players accrue evenly across their game. Plays carry ESPN's wallclock; plays without one borrow a time from their neighbours. Both sides' scores are then replayed in wall-clock order and a win chance is estimated at every play from locked points plus the remaining projection of each unfinished player (normal approximation, spread 0.6 × remaining projection). Slipped Away shows the losing side with the highest peak win chance, the lead at that moment, the opponent's players still to play and what they added from there, with a win-chance chart. The timeline also computes each matchup's point of no return (`blow` on the swing record) but the tab no longer shows it. It waits until the week's game logs are loaded (the tab shows a loading note first), and a matchup is skipped if any scoring starter's game log is missing. The morph patcher keys sibling elements by player id, so the photo button (absolutely positioned above the card) and the name button live in separate wrappers because the League HQ live-refresh patcher keys sibling elements by player id; two buttons with the same id in one parent collapse into one on refresh.


## Record announcements

Immediately after Weekly Awards, full-width record banners compare the book at the end of the selected matchup period with the book before it. The 2026 Week 2 closest finish is Cesar 118.70–Jarrett 118.68 (0.02), beating Wasi 134.36–Jarrett 134.28 in 2022 Week 2 (0.08). The renderer uses the calculated timeline, not a hardcoded result. Each banner includes manager profiles, matchup buttons, the previous mark, and “Added to the League Record Book” linked to its exact entry, such as `#record-book/closest-game`.

All record-book categories can announce a strictly broken record except career regular-season win totals, which keep updating silently. Ties do not announce a broken record. Announcements stay in their original recap when later weeks break the record again; score corrections rebuild and retract invalid announcements. Season rate records require a completed regular season. Postseason recaps include completed bracket games and weeks with byes, and wait for every scheduled two-team matchup to finish. Missing intervening seasons suppress announcements until the historical data is available.
