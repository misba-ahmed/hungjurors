# Automatic Season Challenges

The existing ESPN league sync updates this section at its normal cadence: every 15 seconds during games, 30 seconds near kickoff, and 60 seconds otherwise while the page is visible. No separate manual data entry or workflow dispatch is needed. Opening the site loads all elapsed weeks; switching challenge tabs keeps the same shared results.

`challenge-engine.js` calculates results from each week's ESPN scoring-period roster, actual player stats and historical projections. `challenge-live.js` uses the site's shared request cache and historical-week cache, then redraws the selected challenge. Historical lineups refresh every five minutes to pick up corrections. All totals recompute from the records, so refreshes cannot duplicate awards. Current-week figures are provisional; raffle tickets and eliminations require final scores.

- Raffle: weekly high-score tickets through the league's regular-season endpoint (currently Week 14). Tied high scorers each receive a ticket. Tracking does not invent an end-of-season draw result. The tab keeps the rule text but drops the status line and stat cards: the same illustrated manager lineup appears with the Last Man Standing zoom controls and double-tap toggle on phones (fitted to the width by default, figures capped at the lineup's standard 120px size), each earned ticket floats above its manager's head as a ticket graphic printed with the week and that week's score, and the ticket count with current odds sits under each name. The always-open Weekly Highest Scorer table below it (week and score in bold) comes from the same recomputed ESPN records, so it refreshes with the normal sync. A sync delay still shows the usual warning banner.
- Last Man Standing: eliminate the lowest remaining score starting Week 5. The published rule does not define an elimination tiebreaker. A tied elimination stays unresolved and shows the specific tie; later eliminations wait rather than invent a winner.
- The Last Man Standing lineup uses original avatar heads on individual SVG figures. Final eliminations switch a figure to a seated, greyscale pose. Its chronological feed starts with Week 1, includes the lowest final score during Weeks 1–4 without eliminating anyone, and uses only eligible remaining managers thereafter. Ties or incomplete scores remain pending. The lineup has accessible manager/state names with manager names beneath the baseline; horizontal scrolling keeps figures legible on phones. All challenge manager portraits use the larger shared avatar style. Below the lineup, a titled Weekly Low Score table matches the raffle table: week and score in bold, the lowest manager, and a Safe / Eliminated / Pending pill.
- Titty: starter passing, rushing, receiving, fumble-recovery and return TDs, plus made 50+ yard FGs. Aggregate return-TD and 50+ FG fields take precedence over overlapping component fields. Ties use total passing/rushing/receiving yards from starting QBs, RBs and WRs. The tab shows the shared fitted lineup with a live bar above each head scaled to season totals (bars grow in place and flash when a titty lands during a game), the bold season total under each name, and a crown on the current leader after the yards tiebreak (an exact tie crowns everyone tied). Beneath it, the week-by-week heatmap copies the League Awards card (newest week first, the current week highlighted, provisional live counts included). Every cell is a button: clicking it slides open the shared breakdown row beneath that manager with an arrow to the cell, listing the starters who scored (position, player, count) and the week total. The open breakdown and zoom state survive each live redraw.
- Overachiever: official weekly score minus that week's starters' ESPN projections. The tab shows the shared fitted lineup with the ground line standing in for the projection: a manager over projection rises on a gold pedestal (height by how far over, scaled to the largest total), a manager under projection sinks into a dark pit (depth by how far under, never past the chest), the signed season total floats above each head in green or red, the points behind the current leader sit under each name (Leader for the top spot) and the leader wears the crown. Live redraws animate the rise or sink from the previous position, but the lineup and chart only count final weeks: the week in progress joins after its last game. Beneath it, the Weekly Over/Under chart copies the League Awards card (newest final week first, signed cells shaded green or red, nowrap so totals never overlap names). Each cell is a button that slides open the shared breakdown row with Projected → Actual and the signed difference.
- MVP: compare against the whole ESPN player pool for all six positions, award only if the manager started the winning player, and award the overall bonus. Equal positional highs share the award. Ties use fantasy points from unique MVP players per week (the overall bonus does not duplicate the player's tiebreak points). The tab shows the shared fitted lineup with a bar per manager that counts final weeks only (season MVP points above the bar, the running sum of their MVP players' fantasy points inside it), a crown on the leader after the tiebreak, and collapsible weeks that each hold one horizontally scrolling row of navy-gradient award cards (QB, RB, WR, TE, K, D/ST, Overall) with the ESPN headshot or team logo rising out of the card top, position, player, the starting manager's portrait and name (or On <Manager>'s bench / Free agent), the week's points and that week's opponent (vs/@) from the ESPN scoreboard, which `challenge-live.js` loads per week alongside the league data.
- Lineup shell: the zoom buttons sit inside the stage's top-right corner on phones (30px, with extra top padding so they never cover a bar label), and the figure art paints above the bars so a crown is never hidden.
- Sitewide interaction rule inside the challenges: every player photo or name is a `.pc-player-trigger` that opens the player card, and every manager portrait or name (lineup figures and names included, with a short delay on phones so a double tap still zooms) is a `.manager-profile-trigger` that opens the manager profile.
- Optimizer: maximize actual points under ESPN slot eligibility using that week's full roster, excluding IR. Players can fill only one slot. Empty slots may score zero. Gap compares the optimal lineup with actual starter points, excluding commissioner score adjustments. `hjChOptimalLineup` now keeps the winning assignment (slot per player) and `hjChSwaps` pairs each player who should have started with the starter he replaces, matched by slot, then by position, then in order, so every week carries its own sit/start list. The tab seats every manager on one long bench with a gold bar above each head for season points left behind (the shortest bar wears the crown), then the Weekly Points Left on the Bench grid in the League Awards style (final weeks only, 0.00 cells tinted green, larger gaps redder, nowrap totals); the lineup bars also wait for a week's last game. Clicking a cell slides open a navy breakdown row beneath that manager with an arrow pointing at the cell: one line per swap (slot, the starter struck through with his points, the bench player and his points, the pickup in green), then Actual → Optimal with the total gap, or a Perfect lineup line. One breakdown is open at a time; clicking another cell in the same row swaps the contents, another row moves it, the same cell closes it, and it survives live redraws.

The four stat challenges run through the league's final scoring period (currently Week 16), including playoffs, as the existing archived challenge records do. Missing records remain pending and partial totals are labeled. Failed requests retry with the next sync; successful prior results retain their original retrieval times.

Validation: `node scripts/test-challenges.mjs` exercises scoring, ties, starter eligibility, flex assignments, IR, missing records, season boundaries, and repeat-refresh behavior. The publishing workflow runs it alongside the Vegas feed checks.

ESPN stat identifiers follow the existing player-stat integration and the maintained mapping in https://github.com/cwendt94/espn-api/blob/master/espn_api/football/constant.py.
