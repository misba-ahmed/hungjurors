# Fantasy Football News: data rules (audit, October 19, 2026)

- **Current teams.** The news roster starts from the built-in preseason snapshot (`FFN_BOOTSTRAP_ROSTER`); the live ESPN team-roster refresh is no longer reached by the feed. `scripts/news-feed.js` therefore corrects every player's team from ESPN's fantasy player list (`/players?view=players_wl`, one request, cached 30 minutes in `hj:news-teams:v1:<season>`), marks released players FA, and adds players the snapshot lacks when they are on a league roster or ≥1% owned. Team, logo, game line and "Related" teammates all follow the current team, including on older stories.
- **Live week.** News game lines carry `live:true` and always use the current week (`hjNewsWeek`). Previously a card drawn before league data loaded stored week 1 and kept showing that week's game and stats.
- **Weekly numbers.** One league-scored `kona_player_info` request (ids of the cards on screen, actual + projection for the week; every minute during live games, otherwise every 10 minutes) supplies stats for players who are not on a league roster.
- **Meta line.** `HOU · WR5 · 15.90 PTS`: team, position with the site's position rank (`hjFantasyRankForPlayer`, the same rank as rosters and lineups), then one number — the fantasy score once the game starts (bold, green / orange / red against the ESPN projection), otherwise the ESPN projection in plain italics in the line's own colour (`14.86 proj.`). Nothing when ESPN has no number. Dots are spaced.
- **Did not play.** A news card whose game is final and whose ESPN week record has no non-zero stat says "Did not play" instead of a row of zeros or "Stats pending". Lineup rows elsewhere are unchanged.
- **Search zoom.** The news "Player name" box uses the same rule as the Players tab search (`scripts/player-search.js`): iOS keeps its focus zoom while typing, and the page zooms back out when Enter is pressed or the keyboard closes.
